📢 Products Kaise Promote Karein?

✍️ 1. Website Ya Blog Pe

Apni website pe product reviews likho — jaise "Best Mobile Under Rs.30,000" — aur us mein affiliate links daalo. Google se organic traffic aata rahega aur passive income milti rahegi!

📺 2. YouTube Pe

Kisi product ka review video banao — description mein affiliate link daalo. "Is link se kharidein" likhو — viewers khareedenge aur commission milegi.

💬 3. WhatsApp Groups Mein

Family ya dost ke groups mein useful products share karo — "Ye mujhe bohot acha laga، link ye hai" — simple aur effective!

📱 4. Facebook Groups

Online earning، shopping، tech groups mein genuine recommendations do — link saath mein. Pakistani groups mein lakhon members hain!

⭐ Sabse Best Combo: Website + YouTube — Blog pe SEO se traffic aata hai، YouTube se views aate hain — dono mein same affiliate links — double earning!
