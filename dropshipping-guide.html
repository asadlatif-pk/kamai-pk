💡 Dropshipping Kya Hai?

Dropshipping ek aisa business model hai jisme tum ek online store chalate ho — lekin tumhare paas koi bhi stock ya inventory nahi hoti! Jab customer tumhare store se koi cheez khareedta hai — tum woh order directly supplier ko forward karte ho, aur supplier customer ke ghar tak deliver karta hai.

Tumhara kaam sirf yeh hai: online store banao, customers lao, orders forward karo — aur beech ka profit apni jeb mein rakho!

⚙️ Dropshipping Ka Poora Process — Detail Mein

Yeh samjho ke exactly kya hota hai ek order aane se deliver hone tak:

1

Tum Store Banate Ho

Daraz Seller ya Shopify pe apna online store banate ho. Yeh tumhara "front" hai — customers yahan aate hain.

2

Products List Karte Ho

Supplier ke products ki photos, description aur apna price likh ke store mein add karte ho. Supplier ka price + apna profit = tumhara selling price.

3

Customer Order Karta Hai

Koi customer tumhare store se order karta hai aur payment karta hai. Paise tumhare paas aa jate hain.

4

Tum Supplier Ko Order Karte Ho

Us customer ka address use karke tum supplier (AliExpress/Daraz Wholesale) se same product order karte ho — supplier ke rate pe.

5

Supplier Directly Deliver Karta Hai

Supplier package customer ke ghar bhej deta hai. Tum ne kuch bhi touch nahi kiya!

6

Tumhara Profit — Tum Rakh Lo!

Customer price minus supplier price = TUMHARA PROFIT. Isse "margin" kehte hain. Yahi tumhari kamaai hai!

⚠️ Ek Important Baat: Customer ko pata nahi hota ke yeh dropshipping hai — unhe sirf product milna chahiye on time aur sahi condition mein. Isliye trustworthy suppliers choose karna bahut zaroori hai!

🟠 Method 1: Daraz Dropshipping — Mukammal Guide

Daraz Pakistan ka sabse bada ecommerce platform hai — 3 crore+ active buyers! Yahan dropshipping karna sabse aasan tareeqa hai beginners ke liye.

📋 Daraz Seller Account Kaise Banayein

1

Seller Account Register Karo

seller.daraz.pk pe jao → "Register" pe click karo → CNIC, Mobile Number aur Bank Account details bhar do → Yeh FREE hai!

  • Apna CNIC number ready rakho
  • Pakistani bank account (Meezan, HBL, MCB, Sadapay — koi bhi)
  • Business name sochna hoga — koi bhi professional naam rakho
2

Account Approve Hone Ka Wait Karo

Daraz verification 1–3 business days mein karta hai. Approval email aa jaati hai. Is waqt apni niche aur products research karo.

3

Supplier Dhoondo

Daraz pe hi wholesale sellers hain — "Daraz Mall" aur "Official Store" wale suppliers hote hain. Ya phir local wholesale markets se contact karo:

  • Karachi — Bolton Market, Zainab Market
  • Lahore — Hall Road, Hafeez Centre
  • Online — OLX wholesale, Facebook Wholesale Groups
  • AliExpress — China se direct (shipping 10–20 din)
4

Products List Karo — Sahi Tarike Se

Seller Center mein "Add Product" pe jao. Yeh cheezein zaroori hain ek winning listing ke liye:

  • Title: Main keyword + product name + features — jaise "Wireless Bluetooth Earphones HD Sound Pakistan"
  • Photos: Clear, bright, white background — minimum 5 photos
  • Description: Urdu ya English mein complete — features, uses, dimensions
  • Price: Competitors se thoda kam ya same — pehle orders lene ke liye
  • Category: Sahi category mein add karo — search mein aayega
5

Order Aaye — Process Karo

Jab order aata hai — Seller Center pe notification aati hai. Supplier ko order karo customer ke address pe. Daraz tracking update karo. 3–7 din mein delivery ho jaati hai.

6

Payment Receive Karo

Daraz har 7–14 din mein payment release karta hai directly tumhare bank account mein. Commission 5–15% Daraz kaatta hai — baaki tumhara!

⚠️ Daraz Commission Structure:
Daraz har category mein alag commission leta hai — Electronics: 5–8%, Fashion: 10–15%, Home Decor: 8–12%. Yeh apne margin mein include karo selling price set karte waqt.

💡 Daraz Pe Successful Seller Banne Ke Secrets

  • Fast Dispatch karo — 24 ghante ke andar order dispatch karo. Daraz ka algorithm fast sellers ko zyada visibility deta hai
  • Reviews zaroori hain — customers se politely reviews maango. 4.5+ rating se sales automatic barh jaati hain
  • Daraz Campaigns mein participate karo — 11.11, Eid Sale, Payday Sale — in campaigns mein sales 5–10x ho jaati hain
  • Flexscore improve karo — Daraz ek score deta hai tumhare store ko. Jitna zyada score, utni zyada visibility
  • Return Policy clear rakhna — returns se ghabrao mat — Daraz returns handle karta hai

🔵 Method 2: Shopify + AliExpress — Dollar Mein Kamao

Yeh method thoda advanced hai lekin earning potential bahut zyada hai — international customers se dollar mein kamao!

1

Shopify Account Banao — 3 Din Free Trial

shopify.com pe jao → Free Trial shuru karo → Store setup wizard follow karo. Store name, country (Pakistan), currency set karo.

2

DSers App Install Karo (Free)

Shopify App Store se "DSers" app install karo — yeh AliExpress ke products automatically tumhare store mein import karta hai. Ek click mein order forward ho jaata hai!

3

AliExpress Pe Products Dhoondo

aliexpress.com pe apni niche ke products dhoondo. In cheezeon pe dhyan do:

  • 1000+ orders wale products — prove shuda demand hai
  • 4.5+ rating — quality confirmed
  • "ePacket" shipping available — fast delivery to US/UK
  • High margin products — cost ka kam se kam 2x price pe becho
4

Products Import Karo — DSers Se

DSers Chrome Extension install karo → AliExpress pe product pe jao → "Add to Import List" → DSers mein customize karo → Shopify store mein publish karo. 2 minute ka kaam!

5

Payment Gateway Set Karo

Pakistan se Shopify pe payment accept karna thoda tricky hai lekin possible hai:

  • Stripe: Pakistani account se kaam nahi karta directly
  • 2Checkout / Payoneer Commerce: Pakistan se kaam karta hai
  • Wise Business: US bank account milta hai — Stripe use ho sakta hai
  • Best Option: Payoneer se US virtual account banao → Stripe connect karo
6

Marketing Karo — Customers Lao

Shopify store pe customers khud nahi aate — tumhe marketing karni hogi. Facebook Ads ya TikTok Ads sabse effective hain dropshipping ke liye. Starting budget: $5–$10/day se shuru karo.

💡 Pakistan Se Shopify Ka Sab Se Easy Tareeqa

Shopify ka "Starter Plan" sirf $5/month mein milta hai — pehle isi se shuru karo. Phir jab sales aane lagin tab $29 wale plan pe jao jisme poora store milta hai.

📊 Profit Kaise Calculate Karein?

Bahut se log yeh galti karte hain ke sab expenses count nahi karte — phir loss hota hai. Yeh formula use karo:

💰 Profit Calculation Formula — Example

Selling Price (customer se lo) Rs.2,500
(-) Product Cost (supplier ko do) - Rs.1,200
(-) Platform Commission (Daraz ~10%) - Rs.250
(-) Shipping Cost (agar applicable) - Rs.150
(-) Packaging (agar koi) - Rs.50
= NET PROFIT PER ORDER Rs.850 ✅
🎯 Good Margin Kitna Hona Chahiye?
Pakistan (Daraz) ke liye: 30–50% margin target karo har product pe.
International (Shopify) ke liye: 40–70% margin — ads ki cost bhi hai to zyada chahiye.

🛒 High Margin Products Ki Pehchan

  • Supplier price Rs.200–Rs.500 — Rs.800–Rs.1,500 pe bech sako
  • Zyada weight nahi — shipping cost kam ho
  • Return rate low ho — electronics se door raho
  • Seasonal nahi — year round chale
  • Unique ho — Daraz pe same exact cheez 100 sellers na bech rahe hoon

📢 Store Pe Customers Kaise Layein?

Dropshipping mein sabse bada challenge hai — traffic aur customers laana! Yeh proven methods use karo:

📱 1. Facebook & Instagram Ads (Paid — Fast Results)

Dropshipping mein sabse effective marketing hai Facebook/Instagram Ads. Tum exactly woh log target kar sakte ho jinhe tumhara product chahiye — age, city, interests sab filter hota hai.

  • Pakistan mein starting budget: Rs.500–Rs.1,000/day se shuru karo testing ke liye
  • International ke liye: $5–$10/day se start karo, winning ad milne pe scale karo
  • Video ads zyada effective hain image ads se — product use karte dikhao
  • Pehle broad audience target karo, phir narrow karo winning audience milne pe

🎵 2. TikTok Organic (FREE — Viral Potential)

TikTok pe product videos banao — unboxing, demo, before/after — aur viral ho jao. Ek viral video se hazar orders aa sakte hain bilkul free!

  • Product unboxing + reaction video — sabse zyada viral hota hai
  • "Problem → Solution" format — "Yeh masla tha, yeh product ne solve kiya"
  • Trending sounds use karo — algorithm zyada show karta hai
  • Roz kam az kam 2–3 videos upload karo pehle mahine

💬 3. WhatsApp & Facebook Groups (FREE)

Pakistan mein WhatsApp groups aur Facebook buying/selling groups mein products share karo. Yeh beginners ke liye zero-cost aur effective tareeqa hai.

  • "Buying & Selling Pakistan" Facebook groups mein post karo
  • Apna WhatsApp business number daal ke product promote karo
  • Local city ke groups mein promote karo — trust zyada hota hai

📸 4. Instagram Influencer Marketing

Pakistan mein micro-influencers (10K–100K followers) se Rs.2,000–Rs.10,000 mein apna product promote kara sakte ho. Ek acha post zyada orders dila sakta hai.

🔍 5. Daraz Sponsored Ads (Daraz Ke Liye)

Daraz ka apna advertising platform hai — "Sponsored Products" — jisme tum apna product search results mein upar lakar sales badha sakte ho. Rs.500–Rs.1,000 se start karo.

🚀 Beginners Ka Best Strategy

🚫 Common Galtiyan Jo Nahi Karni

❌ Yeh Galtiyan Karo To Fail Ho Jaoge:
  • Sab kuch sell karne ki koshish karna — ek ya do niche choose karo aur unhi pe focus raho. "Sab bechne wala" kuch nahi bechta
  • Bina test kiye zyada products list karna — pehle 5–10 products test karo, jo chale usse scale karo
  • Cheap quality supplier choose karna — 1 star reviews aur returns se store barbad ho jaata hai
  • Slow shipping ke baare mein customers ko na batana — agar 15 din lagte hain, pehle se batao — surprise mat karo
  • Customer messages ignore karna — unhappy customer = bad review = store ruined
  • Branded products dropship karna — Nike, Apple, Adidas ke fake ya unauthorized products Daraz pe instant ban deta hai
  • First day se ads pe sab paise lagana — pehle organic traffic se test karo, phir paid mein invest karo
  • Ek product fail pe quit karna — average dropshipper 10–20 products test karta hai pehle 1 winner milne se

⚡ Expert Tips — Tezi Se Grow Karo

  • Winning products dhoondne ke liye — Facebook Ad Library check karo — jo ads zyada run ho rahe hain woh products sell ho rahe hain
  • Competitors copy karo smartly — Daraz pe bestseller products dekho, same type ka product list karo better price ya photos ke saath
  • Product photos improve karo — white background, multiple angles — acha photo = zyada clicks = zyada sales
  • Bundle deals banao — 2 products saath mein becho — "earphones + case = Rs.1,500 combo" — average order value barh jaata hai
  • Seasonal opportunities capture karo — Eid, Valentine's, Back to School — in occasions se related products pehle se list karo
  • Customer reviews ka jawab do — achhe aur bure dono ko professionally respond karo — trust banta hai
  • Spy tools use karo — Minea, AdSpy, Dropispy — in tools se pata chalta hai kaunse products ads mein chal rahe hain international market mein
  • WhatsApp Business setup karo — customers directly aapko WhatsApp karein — trust aur sales dono barh jaate hain

❓ Aksar Puchhe Jaane Wale Sawal

Kya Pakistan mein dropshipping legal hai?
Haan, Pakistan mein dropshipping 100% legal business hai. Daraz pe toh baaki sellers ki tarah hi hota hai — tum ek reseller ho. Shopify pe international business karne ke liye koi special license nahi chahiye shuru mein. Jab income stable ho jaye tab NTN register karo — accountant se advice lo.
Kya Daraz dropshipping bilkul free hai?
Haan! Daraz Seller account banana bilkul free hai. Daraz sirf tab commission leta hai jab order complete hoti hai — pehle koi fee nahi. Iska matlab hai ke tum risk-free shuru kar sakte ho — koi upfront investment nahi!
Agar customer product return kare to kya hoga?
Daraz pe returns Daraz khud handle karta hai uski return policy ke hisaab se. Tumhe apne supplier se bhi return/replacement ki condition pehle se clear karni chahiye. AliExpress ke zyada tar sellers dispute mein refund ya replacement de dete hain. Returns se ghabrao mat — yeh business ka hissa hai.
Kya mobile se dropshipping ho sakta hai?
Haan! Daraz Seller Center ka mobile app available hai jisse tum orders manage kar sakte ho, products list kar sakte ho aur analytics dekh sakte ho. Basic operations mobile se ho jate hain, lekin product research aur marketing ke liye laptop zyada convenient hai.
Shopify Pakistan se kaise kaam karta hai — payment ka kya hoga?
Shopify Pakistan mein available hai aur store banana aasan hai. Payment ka main challenge yeh hai ke Pakistan mein Stripe directly support nahi karta. Solution: Payoneer se US virtual bank account lo → Stripe se connect karo. Ya phir 2Checkout ya Paddle use karo jo Pakistan ko support karta hai.
Kya bina experience ke dropshipping shuru kar sakte hain?
Bilkul! Dropshipping specially beginners ke liye hi design ki gayi business model hai. Daraz se shuru karo — woh sab kuch guide karte hain. Pehle 1–2 mahine mein bahut kuch seekhne ko milega real experience se. YouTube pe bhi kaafi tutorials hain.
Ek customer order kare to kya main pehle supplier ko apni jeb se paisa deta hun?
Haan — yeh dropshipping ka ek important point hai. Customer se pehle payment aati hai, phir tum supplier ko order karte ho. Daraz pe customer payment already hoti hai jab order aata hai. Isliye cash flow problem nahi hoti. Agar COD (Cash on Delivery) orders hain to thodi planning chahiye.
Kitne products se shuru karein?
Shuru mein 10–20 products se karo — ek hi niche ke. Zyada products se manage karna mushkil ho jaata hai. Jab kuch products pe sales aani shuru ho, unhe scale karo aur phir products add karte jao. Quality over quantity — 100 average products se behtar hai 20 winning products.
