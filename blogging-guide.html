💡 Blogging Kya Hai?

Blog ek online diary ya website hoti hai jisme tum apne topic ke baare mein articles likhte ho. Jab Google pe log kuch search karte hain — tumhara article unhe nazar aata hai, woh aate hain, Ads dekhtay hain — aur tumhe paise milte hain!

Sochо — ek baar acha article likha, aur woh saalon tak Google pe traffic laata rahe aur paise deta rahe. Yahi hai Blogging ka jadoo — ek baar kaam, baar baar income!

🚀 Blog Shuru Kaise Karein — Step by Step

1

Niche (Topic) Choose Karo

Ek specific topic choose karo jisme tumhara interest ho — Health, Technology, Cooking, Online Earning, Islam, Cricket — koi bhi. Focus zaroori hai!

2

Blogger.com Pe Account Banao

Gmail se login karo, "New Blog" press karo, apna blog name aur address rakho. 5 minute mein ready ho jaata hai — bilkul free!

3

Pehle 10-15 Articles Likho

Har article 800-1200 words ka hona chahiye. Asaan, simple Urdu mein likho. Har article mein ek specific sawal ka jawab do jo log Google pe search karte hain.

4

Custom Domain Lo (Optional but Recommended)

Namecheap ya GoDaddy se .com domain sirf Rs.1,500-2,000/year mein milta hai. Domain se blog professional lagta hai aur AdSense approval bhi jaldi milti hai.

5

Google Search Console Mein Add Karo

search.google.com/search-console pe apna blog add karo taake Google tumhara content index kare aur traffic aana shuru ho.

6

Google AdSense Apply Karo

Jab 20-25 articles ho jaayein aur thoda traffic aane lage — AdSense ke liye apply karo. Approval mein 2-4 hafte lagte hain. Phir Ads shuru, income shuru!

💰 Google AdSense Approval Kaise Milta Hai?

Yeh Pakistan mein sabse bada sawaal hai — yeh zaroori cheezein honi chahiye approval ke liye:

  • Kam se kam 20-25 quality articles — copy paste bilkul nahi
  • About Us, Contact, Privacy Policy pages — zaroor banao
  • Custom domain — .blogspot.com pe approval mushkil hai
  • Blog ki age kam se kam 3-6 mahine honi chahiye
  • Koi copyright content ya images nahi hone chahiye
  • Blog ka design clean aur fast hona chahiye
  • Har article original aur useful hona chahiye
💡 Pro Tip: Privacy Policy page banane ke liye privacypolicygenerator.info use karo — free mein generate ho jaata hai. Yeh AdSense approval ke liye must hai!
⚠️ Reject Ho Gayi? Ghabrao mat — wajah fix karo aur 2-4 hafte baad dobara apply karo. Zyada tar log 2nd ya 3rd try mein approval lete hain.

⚡ Kaamyaabi Ke Zaroori Tips

  • Ek niche pe focus karo — sab kuch likhne wala blog Google mein rank nahi karta
  • Regularity zaroori hai — hafte mein kam se kam 2-3 articles zaroor likho
  • Long articles likho — 1000+ words ke articles Google mein zyada rank karte hain
  • Title mein keyword daalo — jo log search karte hain, wahi likho
  • Image add karo — Free images Pixabay ya Pexels se lo
  • Article share karo — Facebook Groups, WhatsApp mein share karo traffic ke liye
  • ChatGPT se help lo — ideas aur outline ke liye, lekin apni zaban mein likho

🚫 Yeh Galtiyan Mat Karo

  • Doosre blogs se copy paste karna — Google penalty deta hai
  • Sirf 3-5 articles likh ke AdSense apply karna — reject hogi
  • Ek mahine mein result expect karna — blogging mein 3-6 mahine lagte hain
  • Bahut zyada topics pe likhna — niche specific raho
  • Copyright images use karna — legal masla bhi ho sakta hai

❓ Aksar Puchhe Jaane Wale Sawal

Kya Urdu mein blog likhna mushkil hai?
Bilkul nahi! Roman Urdu (English haroof mein Urdu) mein bhi likh sakte ho — bahut log yahi karte hain. Blogger mein Urdu font ka bhi support hai. Jo comfortable lage, wahi use karo.
Kya Blogger se AdSense milta hai?
Haan milta hai! Lekin custom domain (.com ya .pk) lena zaroori hai. Free blogspot.com address se AdSense approval mushkil hoti hai. Domain sirf Rs.1,500-2,000/year mein milta hai — investment worth it hai.
Kitne articles likhne ke baad AdSense apply karein?
Kam se kam 20-25 original articles hone chahiye apply karne se pehle. Quality zyada important hai quantity se. Har article 800+ words ka hona chahiye aur useful information honi chahiye.
Blogging se pehla paisa kab milega?
Average 3-6 mahine lagte hain pehle paise tak. AdSense payment tab milti hai jab tumhara balance $100 ho jaye. Shuru mein traffic build karne mein time lagta hai — patience rakhna zaroori hai.
Kya Mobile se blog likh sakte hain?
Haan bilkul! Blogger ka mobile app available hai Android aur iOS pe. Aur mobile browser se bhi kaam ho jaata hai. Laptop nahi hai to bhi shuru kar sakte ho.
AdSense ka paisa kaise milega Pakistan mein?
Google AdSense Pakistan mein Western Union ya cheque se payment karta tha — ab Wire Transfer bhi available hai. Payoneer se bhi receive kar sakte hain kuch cases mein. $100 threshold hone pe automatic payment hoti hai.
